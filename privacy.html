kahya privacy policy

kahya is used only by its owner. With the owner's consent it reads the owner's own Gmail (read-only) to save attachments to the owner's own computer.

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

kahya's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
